    How to interpret the evidence

    Surveys describe their stated respondents and observation periods. Experimental research answers a specific causal question. Technical documentation describes methods. Practical examples and planning procedures are editorial applications, not measured case studies.

    The source directory matches the article. The chart data CSV records values, units and evidence types for the three original charts.

    Versions and limitations

    The retail chart preserves the original August 18, 2026 Census release. Census subsequently flagged those estimates as no longer the most up to date; the release date remains visible. The AI survey section uses the authors’ accessible AMA recap. Observation dates differ from the source-check date.
