    About and editorial policy

    Learn how Marketing Strategies Wiki uses primary sources, distinguishes research from examples and explains the limits of marketing statistics.

    Purpose and audience

    Marketing Strategies Wiki is an educational reference for U.S. business owners, marketers and students. It explains audience research, positioning, strategic frameworks, channel choices, budgets and measurement.

    Evidence and examples

    Preference is given to original research, public statistical agencies, official technical documentation and established educational sources. Statistics retain their population, period and relevant limitations. Source URLs appear as plain text.

    Planning procedures, comparison tables and business examples are original editorial applications. The campaign contribution chart uses explicitly hypothetical inputs. Source organizations do not endorse this publication.

    Editorial transparency

    The website does not present a ranked provider list, client results, professional credentials or fabricated testimonials. It is not affiliated with Wikipedia, the American Marketing Association, Google or the research organizations cited. Page structure draws on familiar encyclopedia conventions, while the text and charts are original.

    Site operation

    This edition is a static information site with no visitor accounts, contact form, advertising tags or newsletter registration. Page search runs locally. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
